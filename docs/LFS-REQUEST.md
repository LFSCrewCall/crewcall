# Request to LFS (draft)

Send this to the Head of Programmes, or whoever looks after student-led projects, and copy in the data protection lead. Fill in the brackets. Don't send it until you're happy with it.

**Subject:** Crew Call: a student-run crewing calendar. Asking for a quick OK

Dear [name],

I'm [your name], [cohort, e.g. MAF217]. I've built Crew Call, a free website where LFS students post their shoots, mark the days they're free and find crew by role. MA Screenwriting, Film Producing and Film Marketing students can use it too, and screenwriters can post scripts looking for a director or producer.

It's run by students and isn't an LFS service. Before I launch it, I'd like to check five things with you:

1. **Name and look.** It's called "Crew Call (for LFS students)" and uses black and red. It doesn't use the LFS logo. Is that all right, or would you prefer different wording or colours?
2. **The student mass email alias.** Could I send one launch email through it, and a short reminder at the start of each term? Who approves these messages?
3. **Data protection.** It stores names, LFS emails, cohort codes, roles and free days. Emails are hidden unless people choose to share them. Alumni and guests are deleted after 12 months without signing in. I've attached the draft privacy notice. Could the data protection lead check it, and say whether LFS needs to be named in it?
4. **Safety reports.** Anyone can report a profile, project or script to the student admins. For anything about safety, harassment or discrimination, who at LFS should we pass it to, and what's the best contact?
5. **A shared sending mailbox (optional).** Could LFS set up a shared mailbox such as crewcall@lfs.org.uk that Crew Call can send sign-in codes from? It would stay with the school when student admins graduate. If not, we'll use a free sending service.

Sign-in only works with an LFS Microsoft account, or by invite for crew from outside LFS. It costs nothing to run.

You can see it here: [link]. I'm happy to show you in person.

Thanks,
[your name]
[cohort]
